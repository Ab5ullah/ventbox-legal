How VentBox works

Three steps, about a minute. Nothing to set up.

  1. 1

    Write it

    Pick how you feel, then type whatever is on your mind. Messy is fine. Nobody reads it.

  2. 2

    Hold to let go

    Press and hold the button and choose how it goes: shredded, burned, swallowed by a blackhole and more.

  3. 3

    It's gone

    Your words vanish from the screen and from the app. Take a breath and put your phone down.

Questions

The short answers.

What is VentBox?

VentBox (also written Vent Box) is an Android app where you type out frustrations or heavy thoughts, then destroy them with an animation. It's a private place to get things out of your head, with nobody watching and nothing kept.

Is VentBox free?

Yes. You can vent as much as you like for free. VentBox Pro is optional and unlocks more destruction effects, themes and mood insights.

Does VentBox save what I write?

No. Your text is never stored or sent to any server, and it's gone as soon as you destroy it.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. VentBox is currently available on Google Play for Android.

Is VentBox therapy?

No. VentBox isn't therapy or a medical service. It's a simple tool for letting things out. If you're struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a professional.
