Vent Box – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Vent Box, your privacy is our highest priority. We believe that your thoughts and feelings are yours alone.

1. DATA COLLECTION

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the text you write in the Vent section. As soon as you complete the destruction ritual, your text is permanently deleted from your device's memory.

2. LOCAL STORAGE

The only data stored on your device is your Mood History and Streaks. This data is stored locally using Hive and never leaves your device.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and payments. They may collect basic device identifiers and transaction history to ensure your Pro features are active. We do not share your emotional data with any third party.

4. CRASH REPORTING

We may use crash reporting tools to improve app stability. No personal or emotional data is included in crash reports.

5. NO TRACKING

We do not use analytics or tracking pixels to monitor your behavior. Your venting sessions are completely private.

6. GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to request deletion of any locally stored data by uninstalling the app.

7. CONTACT

If you have any questions, please contact us at dinonovalab@gmail.com
